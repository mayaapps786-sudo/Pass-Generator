Privacy Policy

Pass Generator for Android · Effective date: November 13, 2026

In short: Pass Generator works entirely offline. It does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data or any password it generates.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pass Generator app (“the App”) handles information. The App is provided by [MayaApps786] (“we”, “us”, “our”).

Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no forms that ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personal details.

How passwords are generated

Passwords are generated locally on your device, based on the length and character options you choose in the App. The App does not need an internet connection to work.

  • Generated passwords are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Generated passwords are not saved to a server, logged, or backed up by the App.
  • Once you generate a new password or close the App, the previous password is not kept by the App.

Permissions

The App does not request any Android permissions. In particular, it does not request access to the internet, your contacts, files, photos, camera, microphone, or location.

Copying a password to the clipboard

When you tap Copy Password, the App places the current password on your device’s clipboard so that you can paste it where you need it. This only happens when you choose to do it. The clipboard is managed by Android, not by the App, and depending on your device and Android version, other apps or keyboard tools may be able to read clipboard contents. For extra safety, paste the password right away and then clear your clipboard or copy something else.

Analytics, advertising, and third parties

The App contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools, and no tracking technologies. We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any.

Data stored on your device

The App does not keep a history of the passwords it generates. Any basic settings it uses, such as your selected password length or character options, stay on your device only and are never transmitted. Uninstalling the App removes any data it has stored on your device.

Children’s privacy

Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Since we do not collect or hold any personal data about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export, or delete. If you have questions about your privacy rights, you are welcome to contact us using the details below.

Security

Because no data leaves your device through the App, there is no server-side storage that could be breached. You are still responsible for keeping the passwords you generate in a safe place, such as a reputable password manager.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we do, we will post the updated version at this page and change the effective date at the top. If a future version of the App changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
